Excellence with Purpose

KVR Maruthi PU College is an extension of the trusted legacy of Maruthi Education Trust, which has been shaping young minds in Tumkur since 1986.
Established over a decade ago, KVR Maruthi PU College was founded with a single-minded focus on Science education.

Why Choose KVR Maruthi PU College

We foster an environment where bright, curious students are given the best possible foundation for careers in medicine, engineering, research, and beyond. With expert faculty, high-quality coaching, and hands-on lab facilities, we help students compete at the highest levels without losing sight of who they are.

We believe in nurturing independent thinkers who carry forward both academic strength and strong integrity.
