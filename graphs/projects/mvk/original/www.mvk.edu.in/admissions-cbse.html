School Life

We believe students learn best when their day is structured, balanced, and purposeful.
‍
Students are mentored not just academically, but in every aspect of personal development — preparing them for life beyond school and cultivate independent thinking

Our daily schedule includes:

Focused academic sessions
Guided self-study periods.
Regular yoga and wellness classes.
Time for sports and personal reflection.

Life at Maruthi Vidya Kendra

Morning

Students assemble for morning prayer, a few moments of guided meditation, and the national anthem, setting a tone of focus and respect for the day ahead. The students also share daily news to keep themselves updated about their world beyond school.

Afternoon

Classes begin promptly, with teachers leading structured lessons in science, mathematics, languages, and social studies. The afternoon sessions balance academics with enrichment—art, music, yoga, physical education, and moral science.

Evening

Holitic education is woven naturally into the rhythm of the day. There’s time for play, time for reflection, and time for guided self-study before students return home—grounded, cheerful, and ready to continue learning beyond the classroom.

Our Process

Our admissions are open for the academic year 2026–27.
We invite families who believe in balanced, value-based education—one that blends rigorous academics with cultural and spiritual grounding.

Step 1: Enquiry & Visit

Parents may connect with our Admissions Office for initial guidance. You can schedule a visit either at our City or Belagumba campus to experience our classrooms, facilities, and meet our teachers who make learning a holistic experience.

Step 2: Application Submission

Applications must be submitted along with:

A copy of the student’s previous academic record
Transfer Certificate.
Proof of identity (Aadhaar or equivalent).

Our team will provide the full list of required documents upon enquiry.

Step 3: Interaction & Review

We conduct a short interaction with the child and parents to understand the student’s learning style and readiness. Admissions to higher classes are based on merit and seat availability.

Step 4: Admission Confirmation

Selected students will be notified via phone or email. Admission is confirmed upon submission of original documents and payment of the prescribed fee.

Admission is strictly based on merit and seat availability.

FAQs

Can I get a virtual tour of the campus?

Yes. Prospective students and parents can request a virtual tour through the admissions office. We are happy to walk you through our campus facilities, hostels, classrooms, and labs.

What documents are required for admission?

Admission is primarily based on previous academic performance and an interaction with the student and parents. For certain grades, additional assessments may be conducted.

Can students from other boards apply?

Yes. Students from State Board, CBSE, ICSE, or other recognized boards are welcome, subject to seat availability and curriculum equivalence.

Are there hostel facilities?

At our Belagumba campus, hostel facilities are available for students of class 8 and above.
