Our Campuses

Each space is built to support focused learning, personal growth, and a strong sense of belonging — from a child’s first school day to their final board exam. MVK operates across two thoughtfully designed campuses situated in Tumkur, Karnataka.

City Campus

Home to MVK State Board School — centrally located, newly renovated, and rooted in 40 years of community trust.

Belagumba Campus

A peaceful, purpose-built campus surrounded by hills, home to our Kindergarten, CBSE School, and PU College.
