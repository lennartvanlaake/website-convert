About Maruthi Vidya Kendra

At Maruthi Vidya Kendra (MVK), we are shaping the next generation of thinkers, innovators, and changemakers. Established in 1986, MVK has grown from a small school of 15 students into one of Tumkur’s leading educational institutions, recognized for its commitment to academic excellence, character development, and global readiness.

Rooted In Purpose

Our story is rooted in the vision of Late K. Venkataramaiah (KVR), a dedicated educator who believed that true learning extends beyond textbooks. His philosophy continues to inspire us. We cultivate an environment where students are encouraged to question, create, and lead with confidence.

Driven by Wisdom

At MVK, education is a transformative journey. We integrate rigorous academics with real-world learning, empowering students to develop the skills, mindset, and resilience needed to thrive in an ever-changing world. Guided by our values of wisdom, humility, courage, and strength, we prepare learners not just for success but for significance.

Our Commitment

At MVK, we don’t just educate. We inspire. Join us in shaping a future that is bold, bright, and limitless.

Student Centered Learning

Every student is at the heart of what we do, fostering curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning.

Global Perspectives Local Roots

 A curriculum that balances international standards with the rich cultural heritage of India.

Holistic Development

Academics, arts, sports, and leadership programs that shape well-rounded individuals.

A Legacy of Excellence

Built on decades of trust, integrity, and a passion for education.

Ready to Shape Your Future?

A thoughtfully built academic ecosystem where students can complete their education right from their foundation years through to their seconday education.
